# DBMS Project Institutional Standard
## Recruitment Protocol & Technical Assessment

This document outlines the core design principles and architectural identity of the **DBMS Recruitment Protocol**.

### 1. Identity & Branding
- **Project Name**: DBMS Recruitment Protocol (Institutional Standard)
- **Primary Aesthetic**: High-fidelity, clinical, and data-dense.
- **Visual Tokens**:
  - **Base**: Zinc-950 (Operational Black)
  - **Accents**: Siren Amber (#f59e0b) for active alerts, Emerald-500 for status readiness.
  - **Typography**: Plus Jakarta Sans (UI) / JetBrains Mono (Telemetry/Code).

### 2. Design Philosophy: "Operational Command Center"
The system is designed to simulate a high-security institutional interface rather than a standard consumer SaaS platform.
- **Asymmetry**: Purposeful grid layouts to prioritize operational metrics over aesthetic symmetry.
- **Telemetry**: Real-time data visualization using monospace fonts to emphasize technical precision.
- **Dense Information Architecture**: Reducing white space in favor of functional density for expert recruiters and administrators.

### 3. Core Components
- **Institutional Card**: Bordered Zinc-900 containers with monospace headers.
- **Neural Pulse Engine**: AI interviewer visualization with GAZING and LISTENING sensorial states.
- **Recruitment Telemetry**: Terminal-style logs for proctoring and behavior monitoring.

### 4. Rebranding History
- **Legacy Identity**: BoothIQ / SkillSync
- **Correction**: Pivot to "DBMS Project" established 2026-04-14. All future development must strictly adhere to the DBMS Institutional Standard.
